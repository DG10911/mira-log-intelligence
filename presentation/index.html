The Problem

Failures & attacks hide in millions of log lines — and surface too late.

  • Hospital infrastructure (EMR, PACS, LIS, Pharmacy, Billing) emits a relentless stream of logs.
  • Static thresholds like if error_rate > 0.2 are brittle — they false-alarm on normal traffic shifts and miss slow-building problems.
  • By the time a human notices, it's already an outage or a breach.
  • Teams need abnormal behaviour spotted in seconds, with a reason — not a wall of raw logs.
Our Solution

A streaming detection platform that learns, explains, and correlates.

🟢 Learns "normal" itself

Adaptive per-feature baseline (EWMA + robust MAD), contamination-guarded — no hardcoded thresholds.

🔍 Detects in real time

Robust-Z, CUSUM drift, novel-template & a security rule engine, fused into one graded alert.

💬 Explains every alert

“error rate 12%→47%, 4.1σ above baseline” — what · why · how much · where · evidence.

🧩 Correlates incidents

500 alerts become one incident with 500 occurrences and a full lifecycle.

📡 Real-time console

Redis event bus → WebSocket → live dashboard, sub-second.

☁️ Enterprise fan-out

AWS CloudWatch (every detection) + SNS (one alert per new incident).

Architecture

One clean pipeline, end to end.

The Detection Engine · our differentiator

Why it's accurate, not just alarmy.

Adaptive baseline

Dual EWMA (fast + slow) for drift, robust median/MAD, and a contamination guard so anomalies never redefine “normal”.

Effect-size gate

A feature must clear a real change floor, not just a big σ. This single idea cut false positives 91% while recall stayed at 1.0.

CUSUM change-point

Catches slow, sustained drift (5%→8%→12%) that a sliding window misses.

Honest security signals

Brute-force, credential-stuffing, injection — phrased as “possible”, never “confirmed”. Evidence-grounded.
