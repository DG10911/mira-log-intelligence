01

PROBLEM

Hospital systems fail silently.

Hardcoded thresholds

Most tools fire on if error_rate > 0.2. Wrong at 3am, wrong on a holiday, wrong when traffic shifts.

Alert storms, no meaning

Thousands of duplicate alerts, none explaining why. Teams stop looking.

Detected too late

PACS, EHR, lab & pharmacy degradations surface as outages — after patients are affected.

The enemy is the hardcoded threshold. It's brittle and noisy. Land the human stakes: in a hospital, "detected late" means clinical impact.
02

SOLUTION

A baseline that learns itself.

MIRA models "normal" online with an adaptive baseline — EWMA + median/MAD robust z-score, per feature, warm-up gated. No thresholds to set. Every alert ships with a human-readable reason and evidence.

Adaptive baseline Graded severity Explainable Honest signals Real-time
The core idea in one breath: it learns normal by itself and resists spike-poisoning by updating the baseline AFTER detection, skipping anomalous features. "Honest" = we say 'possible brute-force', never 'confirmed attack'.
03

HOW IT WORKS

One streaming pipeline, every second.

Ingest→ Normalize→ Drain3 templates→ 6 windows→ Adaptive baseline→ Detect + secure→ Fusion→ Incidents

Time-based windows

5 · 30 · 60 · 300 · 900 · 3600s

Not count-based — honest under bursty traffic.

~17 features / tick

error/warn rate, 2xx–5xx mix, p50/p95/p99, unique IPs/users, failed logins, template entropy…

Sinks

PostgreSQL · Redis → WebSocket · AWS CloudWatch + SNS.

Walk the arrows left to right. Emphasize the highlighted "Adaptive baseline" — that's the differentiator. Windows are time-based, which matters when request volume swings.
04

DETECTION ENGINE

Four detectors, one graded verdict.

robust_zscore

median/MAD deviation per feature, gated by a minimum effect-size floor so a trivial 0.048→0.052 never fakes "10σ".

novel_template

A never-before-seen Drain3 log signature is an instant anomaly. Very demo-able.

CUSUM

Page's change-point detector — catches slow, sustained drift the window misses.

security rules

Brute-force, credential-stuffing, SQL/command-injection signals — honest "possible", never "confirmed".

Fusion → LOW 2σ · MEDIUM 3σ · HIGH 5σ · CRITICAL 8σ

Four independent detectors fused into a single graded severity. The effect-size floor is the credibility trick judges love — it kills the "everything is 400σ" problem on a fresh idle baseline.
